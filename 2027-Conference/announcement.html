33rd International Joint Conference ESUG

We are delighted to announce that the dates and venue for the 33rd International Joint Conference: ESUG 2027, IWST 2027 and isLoop Summer School are now confirmed!

In 2027, the Smalltalk community will gather in Brussels, Belgium, at the Vrije Universiteit Brussel (VUB).

Please save the following dates:

  • 3–5 July 2027 — isLoop Summer School
  • 6–8 July 2027 — IWST 2027
  • 6–9 July 2027 — ESUG 2027

We are looking forward to bringing the Smalltalk community together again for several days of presentations, discussions, workshops, learning, sharing ideas, meeting old friends and making new ones.

Whether you are a long-time member of the Smalltalk community or have only recently discovered Smalltalk, you are very welcome to join us for what we hope will be a great conference.

Save the dates: 3–9 July 2027, Brussels!

More information about the program, registration and practical arrangements will follow.

We hope to see many of you at the VUB in July 2027!

Best regards,

The ESUG 2027 Organizing Team
