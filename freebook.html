Free Books

New Smalltalk books

ESUG sponsors book writing and publishing. In general we strongly favor open-access books on open-source Smalltalks.

  • The Pharo open-access books available at https://books.pharo.org have been sponsored.
  • ESUG sponsored Squeak by Example (http://www.squeakbyexample.org)
  • Andres Valloud Books (Hashing in Smalltalk: theory and practice / A mentoring cours on Smalltalk) at ESUG 2009.
