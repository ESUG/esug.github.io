Getting to Gdansk

**Tram**

  • diagram of tram connections: [ PDF ],
  • tram lines going from the city centre: 6, 9, 12,
  • For the conference venur you should get off at one of the following stops: "Politechnika" or "Miszewskiego"

**SKM train**

  • For the conference venue you should get off at the "Gdańsk Politechnika" station
  • if you want to catch the SKM train at a larger station (e.g., Gdańsk Główny), make sure you are waiting on the platform that handles SKM trains

**Gdańsk airport**

  • The most convenient transport from the Gdańsk airport is to use the train connecting the “Gdańsk Port Lotniczy” station to the “Gdańsk Wrzeszcz” station (it takes about 25 minutes)
  • Below is the list of recommended carriers (taxis are marked with phone numbers):
