YouTube to Pharo

  • Submitted by: Kasper Østerbye, retired
  • Keywords: Converting YouTube videos to text, text can then be read inside Pharo"
  • More Info:

- I made a small (5min) video of what I have made: https://maarumlam.dk/kasper/ESUG2026Kasper.mp4 - The essence is that it is possible to convert a YouTube video to text using a Pharo tool I have build.
