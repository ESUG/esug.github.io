ESUG 2026 International Conference

The 31th ESUG conference will be held in Plovdiv, Bulgaria from Tuesday 7th to Friday 10th of July 2026. The collocated isLoop summer school to be held from Saturday 4th to Monday 6th of July 2026 and spread all during the conference through student volunteering program.
