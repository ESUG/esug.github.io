Innovation Technology Awards 21th Edition

Call for the Innovation Technology Awards

Have you developed a cool Smalltalk project you want to share? Developers of any Smalltalk-based software can participate.

Submit now!!!

Submission Deadline: July 3rd

Before the conference, approved projects will be listed on a page dedicated to submissions. During the conference, participants demonstrate their systems in a session Tuesday July 7th, during which the conference attendees vote. A vote consists in providing a sorted list of 3 favorite demonstrated software.

The top 3 teams with the most innovative software will receive a certificate and financial prize of 500 Euros, 300 Euros and 200 Euros, respectively. The results will be announced in the Awards ceremony that is held during the social event.

Entries and winner will be listed on the dedicated page.
