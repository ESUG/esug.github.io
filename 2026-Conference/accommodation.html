Grand Hotel Discount

This year, our local organizer arranged a discount for accommodation in one of the largest hotels in Plovdiv, the Grand Hotel Plovdiv. The offered prices will be different for different room types with included breakfast.

Discount Conditions

CLASIC Room

  • Single Room: 56.24 EUR;
  • Double Room: 76.69 EUR;

COMFORT Room

  • Single Room: 65.96 EUR;
  • Double Room: 87.43 EUR;

LUXURY Room:

  • Single Room: 74.65 EUR;
  • Double Room: 100.21 EUR;

The proposed prices include:

  • Bed and breakfast;
  • Use of WiFi Internet;
  • 9% VAT,
  • tourist tax;

The proposed prices do not include: Parking: 10.23 euro/car/bed

Check-in after 3:00 PM and check-out by 11:00 AM

IMPORTANT: to use this commercial discount from regular prices you need to make the booking through the hotel website and apply the discount code ESUG.

  • The discount is valid for the period between 03.07. and 12.07.2026.
  • Deadline for reservations with the discount: June 3, 2026.
  • Conditions for payment, changes and cancellations on the reservation:

100% advance payment when making a reservation - by credit card or bank transfer.

    • Changes and cancellations of the given reservation are accepted without penalty until May 3, 2026.
    • Changes and cancellations on the given reservation from 04.05. until 03.06.2026 - 50% of the amount paid will be withheld.
    • Changes and cancellations on the given reservation from 04.06. until 03.07.2026 - 100% of the amount paid will be withheld.

In the case of a No Show, 100% of the sum of the requested reservation is deducted.

How to book with the discount

To initiate the booking process at the Hotel website you should enter the dates and select the room type (see the following figure). Please, note that we have the discount offer only only for the rooms (Classic, Comfort, and Luxury). At this you can already enter the discount code ESUG.

Once you have selected the room type and you are sure that your code is applied, you can see the offer listed as Rate plan under promo code and marked as CONVENTION (see the figure below). If you can't see the offer, please double-check if the code has been successfully applied.
